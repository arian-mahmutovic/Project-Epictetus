FAQ

Who is Project Epictetus for?

Students and young builders who want to accomplish more, but don't have a community to push them.

Is it a social media platform?

No. It is designed around meaningful projects and collaboration.

Is it safe for highschoolers?

Yes. With the platform being designed specially for students, safety is our top priority. There will be no private messages and no unauthorized links. Everything will be moderated hands-on.

Is that actually what it's going to look like?

The platform is currently being developed. This preview represents the experience we are building toward.

What's the vision?

To create a prestigious network for talented students who refuse to be held back by where they are or who they know. We plan to have community events, sponsored competitions, and top-college partnerships.
